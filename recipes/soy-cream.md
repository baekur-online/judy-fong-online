# Soy Milk or Creamed Soy milk of Soy cream

## Ingredients
- [ ] dried soy beans
- [ ] water
- [ ] rock sugar

## Aðferð
1. Soak beans in doubke to three times the amount of water for 6 hours or more in the fridge.
2. Blend beans in blender.
3. Boil beans in one part beans amd ten parts water stirring constantly. The mixture must have noticable bubbles.
4. When its been boiling not just foaming for five minutes youll smell the soy milk scent. Boil for ten minutes more for a total of 15 mins.
5. Add rock sugar to taste.
6. Strain out solids to make cookies or stir fries.
